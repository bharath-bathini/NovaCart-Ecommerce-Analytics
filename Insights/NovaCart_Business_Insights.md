# NovaCart-Business Insights and Recommendations

## 1. Purpose

This document summarizes the key business findings from the NovaCart e-commerce analytics project. The analysis uses synthetic data generated for educational and portfolio purposes.

## 3. Year-over-Year Performance

| Metric | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Total Orders | 17,763 | 32,237 |
| Net Revenue | ₹1,700,350,287.04 | ₹3,100,010,257.55 |
| Total Profit | ₹213,353,632.25 | ₹385,549,541.05 |
| Profit Margin | 12.55% | 12.44% |
### Findings

- Revenue increased by approximately 82.32% between 2024 and 2025.
- Profit increased by approximately 80.71%.
- Profit margin decreased by approximately 0.11 percentage points.

Revenue and profit increased, while profit margin decreased slightly. This makes it important to monitor profitability alongside growth.

## 4. Product Category Performance

The Sports category recorded the highest revenue and profit among the analyzed categories.

| Metric | Sports Category |
|---|---:|
| Revenue | ₹1,075,144,069.21 |
| Profit | ₹146,887,553.88 |
| Profit Margin | 13.66% |
| Return Rate | 8.27% |

The Sports category's return rate was approximately 0.27 percentage points higher than the overall rate of 8.00%.

## 5. Customer Returns

- The overall return rate was 8.00%.
- Size Issue was the most common return reason overall.
- Damaged Product was the most common return reason within the Sports category.

### Recommendations

1. Identify Sports products with high damaged-product return counts.
2. Investigate packaging, handling, and product quality as possible contributors.
3. Examine size-related returns by product and subcategory.
4. Track return rates over time to identify changing patterns.

These are investigation priorities, not confirmed causes of returns.

## 6. Regional and Payment Findings

- The East region recorded the highest revenue.
- Net Banking was the most-used payment method.

Further analysis could compare regional category performance and examine how payment preferences vary across customer segments.

## 7. Recommended Business Actions

1. **Protect profitability:** Monitor profit margin alongside revenue and profit growth.
2. **Investigate returns:** Analyze high-return products and reasons before making operational changes.
3. **Review category performance:** Track revenue, profit, margin, and return rate together.
4. **Monitor regional results:** Compare performance by region and time period.
5. **Use regular reporting:** Review the Power BI dashboard to monitor KPIs and investigate changes.

## 8. Limitations

- The datasets are synthetic and do not represent real customer or business transactions.
- Descriptive analysis identifies patterns but does not establish causation.
- Recommendations require further investigation before being treated as operational decisions.

## 9. Tools Used

## 10. Conclusion

The NovaCart project demonstrates an end-to-end analytics workflow, from synthetic data generation and validation to statistical analysis, SQL reporting, and Power BI visualization.

The findings highlight revenue growth, category performance, regional revenue differences, and opportunities to investigate product returns. The project demonstrates how data analysis can support evidence-based business decisions.
